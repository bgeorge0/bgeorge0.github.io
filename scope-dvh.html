Distance from a DVH objective

Instead of penalising every voxel, an objective can penalise the amount by which a particular DVH point fails. This example measures the horizontal dose shortfall or excess at a selected volume. The curve uses 100 equal-volume synthetic voxels.

Target penalty = w × max(0, Dgoal − Dv)²
OAR penalty = w × max(0, Dv − Dgoal)²

Dv is the dose received by at least v% of the voxels (rank-based, without interpolation).

Try it: Move the dose offset until the penalty first becomes zero. Now change the volume from 95% to 5% and explain why the same dose distribution has a different objective cost. A DVH point alone cannot tell you where a hotspot is.
