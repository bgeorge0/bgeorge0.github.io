Why a low-cost plan can be too baggy

A target-only objective does not directly penalise spill outside the PTV. Here a synthetic radial dose profile combines a broad component and a narrow component. The broad component is more uniform across the target but exposes more normal tissue. Add a ring penalty to change the optimum.

D(r) = (1 − b)Dnarrow(r) + bDbroad(r)
F = meanPTV[(D − 50)²] + w × meanring[max(0, D − L)²]

Equal radial samples, not volumetric voxels. PTV: 0–2 cm; ring: 2–4 cm.
