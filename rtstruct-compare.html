Calculation method and limitations

Dice = 2 × volume(A ∩ B) / (volume(A) + volume(B)). Dice = 1 for identical sampled masks and 0 for disjoint masks. Dice difference = 1 − Dice, not a distance in mm.

  • Both structures are sampled at voxel centres on a common isotropic grid in contour A’s coordinates after any centroid alignment. No image series is required.
  • Axial closed contours are filled by polygon scanlines. CLOSED_PLANAR polygons are combined by union, with even–odd filling of keyhole polygons; CLOSEDPLANAR_XOR uses XOR. Non-standard nested-hole conventions are not inferred.
  • Each contour plane is a slab extending halfway to adjacent planes; end slabs use half the median spacing. Gaps greater than 1.5 times median spacing are not fully bridged. No validated TPS interpolation or end-cap model is used.
  • Grid resolution and slice approximation affect results. Compare finer resolutions to assess sensitivity. When Frame of Reference UIDs differ or are missing, the filled-volume centroids are estimated from the sampled XY masks and slab thickness, then B is translated by centroid(A) − centroid(B) before comparison. This is a three-degree-of-freedom rigid translation; no rotation, scaling or deformation is estimated. Matching UIDs are compared in their original coordinates. Matching UIDs do not prove anatomical registration, and centroid alignment removes positional differences rather than establishing anatomical correspondence.
  • Supported: DICOM Part 10 RTSTRUCT, uncompressed implicit/explicit VR little endian or explicit VR big endian; axial closed contours. Oblique, non-planar, open contours and compressed/deflated datasets are unsupported. Not validated against a clinical TPS.
