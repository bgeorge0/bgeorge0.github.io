Keep the high dose close to the target

A plan can cover the PTV but still spread dose into surrounding tissue. A ring is an extra structure around the target. Giving the ring a dose objective tells the optimiser to reduce the dose outside the target, where possible.

The dotted green line shows the outer edge of the ring. The ring does not include the PTV. Both plans use the same beam options and target goals.

Optional: the maths behind this

The added term is the average squared dose excess within the ring, multiplied by its priority. Other objectives are unchanged. Narrower and wider beam components give the optimiser options for balancing target coverage and dose fall-off.

Ring penalty = priority × mean[max(0, ring dose − goal)²]
