Navigate a Pareto front

Imagine several synthetic thoracic beam arrangements, all giving the same 50 Gy target dose. Their cord and lung doses differ. A plan is dominated if another gives no higher dose to either organ and a lower dose to at least one. The remaining plans form a discrete approximation to the Pareto front.

The target is held fixed. Extra exposure represents an inefficient arrangement with identical target dose, not a deliverable beam model.
