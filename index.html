Applied decision-making

Optimise and normalise: workflow exercises

Six Operator 1 exercises: normalise to maximum dose; assess mandatory OARs, PTVhigh and whole-PTV coverage on the adapted plan itself; then compare treatment intent with the original only when the candidate merits review. Follow the live workflow diagrams, practise re-optimisation loops and name the candidate plan.
