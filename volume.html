Not all dose objectives mean the same thing

A structure contains many voxels. A cumulative dose–volume histogram (DVH) shows the fraction receiving at least each dose. A maximum-dose penalty, a mean-dose objective and a dose–volume objective can respond very differently to the same distribution.

The first group is kept at least as hot as the second group. Equal volume voxels are assumed.

Try it: Set the hot group to 60 Gy, the rest to 5 Gy and threshold to 30 Gy. The mean can be low despite a hotspot. Move the threshold across 60 Gy and observe the abrupt change in V. Direct volume penalties can be non-smooth or discontinuous; practical optimisation often uses surrogate formulations. This DVH metric is for evaluation, not the differentiable objective used in the next lab.
